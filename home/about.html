Our Story

Redefining nail care in the heart of Leticia Homes.

A Sanctuary for Self-Care

Bléum Nails is more than just a nail studio; it is a private sanctuary. As a premier home-based studio, we are dedicated to redefining the nail care experience by combining the intimacy of a private space with the luxury of a high-end salon.

We believe that beauty lies in the details. That is why we specialize in meticulous soft gel extensions and structured manicures designed not just to look beautiful, but to last.

Operating strictly by appointment allows us to ensure that every client receives our undivided attention, premium product application, and a flawless finish that stands the test of time.

Our Core Values

Quality First

We use only premium, curated soft gel systems and polishes to ensure the health and integrity of your natural nails.

Strict Hygiene

Your safety is paramount. All tools are sterilized, and files are single-use to maintain the highest sanitation standards.

Private Comfort

Enjoy your treatment in a quiet, home-based setting free from the noise and rush of commercial salons.
